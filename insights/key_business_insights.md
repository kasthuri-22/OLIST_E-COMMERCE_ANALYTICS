# Key Business Insights

## 1. Customer Retention

### Low repeat purchase rate

* Total delivered customers: **93,358**
* One-time customers: **90,557**
* Repeat customers: **2,801**
* Repeat purchase rate: **~3.00%**

**Insight:**
Only ~3% of customers made more than one delivered purchase, indicating very low repeat purchasing in the observed dataset.

---

## 2. Revenue & Customer Behaviour

### Repeat customers contributed 5.51% of revenue

* One-time customers: **94.49% of revenue**
* Repeat customers: **5.51% of revenue**

**Insight:**
Although repeat customers represented only ~3% of customers, they contributed 5.51% of total revenue. The marketplace was therefore heavily dependent on one-time customers.

---

## 3. Delivery & Customer Experience

### Late deliveries were strongly associated with poorer reviews

| Delivery Status | Average Review Score |
| --------------- | -------------------: |
| On-time         |                 4.29 |
| Late            |                 2.57 |

* Difference: **1.72 stars**

Poor review rate (1–2 stars):

* On-time: **9.23%**
* Late: **54.03%**

**Insight:**
Late deliveries were strongly associated with poorer customer reviews. Late orders averaged 2.57 stars compared with 4.29 stars for on-time orders.

> This indicates an association, not causation.

---

## 4. Operational Performance

### 8.1% of delivered orders were late

* Delivered orders: **96,470**
* Late deliveries: **7,826**
* Late delivery rate: **8.11%**

**Insight:**
Approximately 8.1% of delivered orders experienced delivery delays.

Combined with the review analysis, this suggests that delivery performance is an important area to monitor for customer experience.

---

## 5. Revenue & Product Categories

### Revenue was concentrated in several major categories

Top revenue-generating categories included:

| Category               | Revenue |
| ---------------------- | ------: |
| beleza_saude           | R$1.23M |
| relogios_presentes     | R$1.17M |
| cama_mesa_banho        | R$1.02M |
| esporte_lazer          | R$0.95M |
| informatica_acessorios | R$0.89M |

**Insight:**
Health & beauty, watches & gifts, bed/bath/table, sports & leisure, and computers/accessories were among the strongest revenue-generating categories.

---

## 6. Revenue vs Order Value

### High revenue does not always mean high average order value

Some categories had relatively high average order values despite lower sales volumes:

* `pcs` → approximately **R$1,235**
* `instrumentos_musicais` → approximately **R$302**
* `eletroportateis` → approximately **R$300**

**Insight:**
Category performance was driven by different combinations of order volume and order value. Therefore, revenue alone does not explain the underlying sales pattern.

---

## 7. Geographic Sales

### São Paulo was the dominant market

* São Paulo revenue: **~R$5.07M**
* Rio de Janeiro: **~R$1.76M**
* Minas Gerais: **~R$1.55M**

**Insight:**
São Paulo was the dominant state market, generating approximately R$5.07M in sales and substantially outperforming other states.

---

## 8. Customer Experience

### Customer satisfaction varied across product categories

Highest-rated categories included:

* `livros_interesse_geral` → **4.45**
* `livros_tecnicos` → **4.37**
* `malas_acessorios` → **4.32**
* `alimentos_bebidas` → **4.32**

Lower-rated categories included:

* `moveis_escritorio` → **3.49**
* `fashion_roupa_masculina` → **3.64**
* `telefonia_fixa` → **3.68**

**Insight:**
Customer satisfaction varied considerably across product categories, with office furniture among the lowest-rated categories.

---

## 9. Repeat Purchase Timing

### Returning customers had an average purchase gap of ~79 days

* Average gap between consecutive delivered purchases: **79.15 days**
* Approximately **2.6 months**

**Insight:**
Customers who returned made their next delivered purchase after an average gap of approximately 79 days.

> This does not mean customers churn after 79 days. It represents the average gap among customers who actually returned.

---

## 10. Cohort Retention

### Month-1 retention was very low

Cohort analysis showed that M1 retention was generally **below 1%** across the major cohorts.

Examples:

* Apr 2017 → **0.62%**
* Aug 2017 → **0.69%**
* Sep 2017 → **0.70%**
* Oct 2017 → **0.72%**

**Insight:**
Cohort analysis showed very low month-1 retention across major cohorts, reinforcing the broader finding that repeat customer engagement was weak.

> Newer cohorts have shorter observation periods and should not be directly compared with older cohorts for long-term retention.

---

# Overall Business Takeaways

The analysis suggests five major themes:

### 1. Customer retention is the biggest concern

Only ~3% of customers made repeat purchases.

### 2. Revenue is heavily dependent on first-time buyers

Repeat customers contributed only 5.51% of total revenue.

### 3. Delivery performance is closely linked with customer experience

Late orders had substantially lower average review scores and a much higher proportion of poor reviews.

### 4. Sales are geographically concentrated

São Paulo was by far the strongest state market.

### 5. Product performance varies considerably

Different categories show different combinations of revenue, order volume, order value, and customer satisfaction.

---

## Portfolio Summary

> **The Olist analysis revealed a marketplace with strong sales concentration but weak customer retention. While a small group of product categories and geographic markets generated substantial revenue, only ~3% of customers made repeat purchases. Delivery performance also showed a strong association with customer satisfaction, with late orders receiving significantly lower review scores. These findings highlight customer retention, delivery reliability, and category-level performance as key areas for further investigation.**
