# Olist E-Commerce Analytics

SQL and Power BI analysis of the Brazilian Olist e-commerce dataset to understand sales, customer behaviour, delivery performance, and customer experience.

## Objectives

- Analyze revenue and sales performance
- Understand customer purchasing behaviour
- Evaluate delivery and operational performance
- Analyze customer reviews and experience
- Identify repeat-purchase and retention patterns

## Tools

**MySQL · Power BI · DAX · Excel**

## Key Insights

- **Delivered Revenue:** R$13.22M
- **Repeat Customers:** 2,801
- **Repeat Purchase Rate:** ~3%
- **Late Delivery Rate:** 8.11%
- **Average Delivery Time:** 12.5 days
- **Average Review Score:** 4.09 / 5
- **Late Delivery vs On-Time Rating:** 2.57 vs 4.29
